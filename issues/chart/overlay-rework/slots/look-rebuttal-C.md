# Look rebuttal, slot C, 2026-10-13

Disagreements only:

1. B stepping the main text up (editor body and query at heading 16,
   nothing essential at 10-11) as the default. The Measurement already
   found varhop's sizes sit at Emojis' title and heading values and
   named family and style as the noticed difference. Stepping sizes up
   in the same change as the family swap conflates the two: if text
   still reads wrong afterwards, nobody can say which lever did it.
   It is also not free in the editor. Monospace at heading inside a
   560-wide card fits materially fewer characters per line than title
   does, so long prompts wrap and scroll more. Snap to today's token
   equivalents first, show the operator that screenshot, and step up
   only on their word.

2. The rejected list attributes "a raise of global base-size as part of
   this leaf" to A, B and C. I did not reject a base-size raise. I
   rejected varhop choosing size policy silently (multiplier, step-up),
   and listed a global base-size raise as the operator's own lever if
   snapped sizes still read small. Those are different positions: the
   raise is outside the leaf, not foreclosed by it.

3. A's after-merge step of writing `[menu] background-alpha = 0.95`
   into the operator's ~/.config/omarchy/shell.toml during charting,
   even with approval. Two problems. At 0.95 over a 0.5 scrim the card
   moves only slightly toward a dimmed desktop, so the screenshot will
   likely read as unchanged and invites a second round of guessing at
   a lower value. And setting it before handoff makes 0.95 the baseline
   every successor look proof is shot against, across all menu
   surfaces; if the operator later reverts it as a personal tweak,
   that evidence is stale. The lever should stay operator-owned and
   timed by them, with successor proofs at the theme value unless the
   operator has already taken the lever before handoff and says so.
